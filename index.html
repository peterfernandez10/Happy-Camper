Specialized Repairs

The big three done right, in place.

Some repairs decide an RV's next decade. These are the ones Happy Camper is built around assessed honestly, executed end-to-end, finished like it never happened.

01

Rubber roofs, repaired & replaced in place.

The roof is your RV's umbrella and the most common place quiet damage begins. Happy Camper reseals, patches, or fully replaces rubber roofing right where the rig sits, including the seals around every vent and skylight.

  • Seam & sealant renewal
  • Membrane patching
  • Full replacement, done in place
  • Vent & skylight resealing
Ask about roof work
02

Water damage, made to disappear.

Water never waits and neither should the repair. We track the leak, open the damaged area, dry and rebuild the structure, and match the finishes. The goal: you can't find where it happened.

  • Moisture survey & leak tracking
  • Rot & delamination tear-out
  • Structural rebuild, studs to skin
  • Wall & ceiling finishes matched
Ask about restoration
03

Slide-outs that glide and seal.

A stuck slide-out is a trip-ender. Happy Camper restores mechanisms, replaces worn seals, refits walls and floors, and gets the room moving like new smooth, square, and dry.

  • Mechanism service & alignment
  • Seal & wiper replacement
  • Wall & floor refit
  • Squeak-free, watertight operation
Ask about slide-out service
